## What this is

A test set of everyday underwriting work on nine lines of business in Ohio. Each of its 40 tasks gives the AI the filed rate and rule documents it needs and states the facts in the question. The AI works out exact values: a premium, a factor, a rule, or the errors in a filed manual. The tasks fall into ten kinds of work, four of each, such as a full premium, schedule rating and return premium on a cancellation. They grade 199 fields in all. As their original sets rate them, 12 are easy, 19 medium and 9 hard.

This is a draft. No underwriter, and nobody at any of the insurers, has checked the answers.

## Where the text comes from

Every task is copied unchanged from one of nine of our other open test sets, each built on public rate and rule filings from a state insurance department's public filing system. Each task brings the filed documents of the set it comes from, as that set ships them. Only its name, its tags and the hidden marker line in its question differ.

- Professional liability (errors and omissions): 8 from eo-oh.
- Commercial property: 6 from cp-oh.
- Commercial general liability: 6 from gl-oh.
- Title: 5 from title-oh.
- Contract surety: 4 from surety-oh.
- Medical professional liability: 4 from medmal-oh.
- Private passenger auto: 3 from personal-auto-oh.
- Pet: 2 from pet-oh.
- Farm property and liability: 2 from farm-oh.

The filings were turned from PDF into text with pdftotext and are as filed, errors included, apart from the changes each set records line by line. Personal data is blacked out where a filing had any, such as a signer's name, an e-mail address or a phone number. Company, brand, vendor and agency names are replaced by labels that stay the same within a set, such as [Company A] or [Program A]. The same label names a different company in each set. Several sets also replace the filer's form numbers with neutral codes. We keep no list that links a label or code to the real one. None of the filers has reviewed or endorsed this test set, and we have no tie to them.

## How the answers were worked out

We wrote the questions in the sets the tasks come from. Their facts describe no real risk or person, and every answer follows from the filed text and those facts. Each answer key was worked out step by step from the filed pages, and a check confirms that every key matches that working. Each set's own cover says how its answers were checked again.

## What it does not cover

- Nobody in the trade has reviewed it: no underwriter, and nobody at any of the insurers. Where a filing is unclear, you may disagree with a reading an answer key takes.
- All the filings are Ohio filings, from one or two insurers in each line, for small commercial and personal risks.
- The lines are uneven. Professional liability (errors and omissions) has 8, commercial property 6, commercial general liability 6 and title 5, while pet and farm have 2 each.
- Four tasks for each kind of work show where an AI stands. They are not enough to rank two close results.
- The tasks are also in the sets they come from. A score on one of those sets can include tasks an AI has already seen here.
- It is not underwriting advice. Don't use it to rate real risks.
